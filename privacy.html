1. Information we may receive

When you contact Nakoda Creative Studio, you may choose to provide your name, business name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, city, state, business type, requested services, and project details. Please provide only information relevant to your enquiry.

2. How the enquiry form works

When you submit the enquiry form, this website prepares a message and opens WhatsApp with the details you entered. The website does not automatically send the message, and the enquiry form does not submit information to a website database. Review the message and send it yourself if you want to contact us. Once sent, the information is handled through WhatsApp and may be available to the recipient account.

3. How we use enquiry information

If you send us an enquiry, we use the information you provide to respond, understand your requirements, discuss project scope and pricing, prepare proposals, and communicate about potential or active work. We do not state that submitting a form creates a client relationship or confirms a booking.

4. Website storage and security

The enquiry form is designed to open a WhatsApp draft rather than store submissions on our website hosting server. However, the website host, browser, device, WhatsApp, email providers, and other services you use may process technical or communication data under their own policies. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell enquiry details. We may receive or communicate information through service providers you choose to use, such as WhatsApp or email. We do not control how third-party platforms collect, use, retain, or secure data, so please review their current privacy policies before using them.

6. Cookies and external links

This website links to third-party services including Google and social networks. When you follow those links, the third party's terms and privacy practices apply. We have not described third-party data practices as if they were controlled by Nakoda Creative Studio.

7. Your choices

You can choose not to submit the enquiry form and instead contact us directly by email or WhatsApp. If you have questions about information you have sent us, contact us using the details below. Any request concerning information held by a third-party service may need to be directed to that service.

8. Children

This website and our business enquiry process are intended for people contacting us about business or professional services, not for children to submit personal information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website, services, or enquiry process changes. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.

10. Contact us

For privacy questions, contact Nakoda Creative Studio at hello@nakodacreativestudio.com or WhatsApp +91 98900 53179.
